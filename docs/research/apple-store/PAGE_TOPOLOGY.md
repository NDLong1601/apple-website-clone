# Apple Store Page Topology

Target URL: `https://www.apple.com/store`

Reference screenshots:
- Desktop: `docs/design-references/apple-store/apple-store-desktop-full.png`
- Mobile: `docs/design-references/apple-store/apple-store-mobile-full.png`

## Ordered Sections

1. Global navigation
   - Fixed top bar, `44px` high desktop, translucent white.
   - Closed mega menu state only.

2. Financing ribbon
   - Thin centered strip under nav: "Pay over time...".

3. Store header
   - Large H1: "Store. The best way to buy the products you love."
   - Right support module with Specialist and Store links.

4. Product family rail
   - Horizontal icon rail: Mac, iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Apple Vision Pro, AirPods, AirTag, Apple TV 4K, HomePod, Accessories, Apple Gift Card.

5. Latest rail
   - Large `400x500` cards with image backgrounds and text overlay.
   - First cards: iPhone 17 Pro, MacBook Neo, iPhone 17, Apple Watch Series 11.

6. Help rail
   - Mixed `480x500` and `400x500` cards.
   - Specialist, Specialist video, Apple Intelligence at Apple Store, Today at Apple, Personal Setup.

7. Store difference rail
   - Compact white cards, icons, green/blue/purple accent text.

8. Accessories rail
   - Hero accessory card plus product cards.

9. Audio rail
   - Apple Music promo and product cards.

10. Experience rail
   - Apple Intelligence, Apple Camp, Continuity, AppleCare, Store app.

11. Savings rail
   - Carrier deals, education, refurbished, small business.

12. Quick links and footer
   - Pill quick links and long light-gray legal/directory footer.
